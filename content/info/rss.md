This website provides an RSS feed. Subscribing to the feed enables you
to receive notifications and read the website offline.

## What is RSS?

RSS is an open [web feed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Web_feed)
format, initially released in 1999. The specification is authored by
[RSS Advisory Board](https://www.rssboard.org) and
offered under the terms of the
[CC BY-SA 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/1.0/) license.


## Why?

Being an open format, it is inclusive toward anyone who wishes to use
it. It is decoupled from the profit motive that so frequently ruins
the software we rely on in our daily lives. On corporate social media,
we face censorship, surveillance, lock-in, manipulation, and the
ever-present threat of access being revoked. An RSS feed is entirely
controlled and owned by the individual(s) who operate it. This makes
it immune to the enshittification facing *all* corporate-owned
platforms.
