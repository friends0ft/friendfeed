There are so many options, but I've selected just a couple for each
relevant platform. You probably already have something installed for
RSS and calendars already, but if it came with your phone/computer,
it's probably not a good option.

### About Open Source

While the RSS, ATOM, and Calendar feeds are compatible with Google and
Apple software products, it is *highly* recommended to install better
clients for any feeds you wish to consume. Google and Apple software
products subject you to surveillance, manipulation, advertising,
restriction, and a wide variety of other forms of abuse.

[Free and open source software](https://wikipedia.org/wiki/Free_and_open-source_software)
(FOSS) subjects you to none of these offenses, while offering a more
complete feature set, verifiable transparency, and much wider
compatibility. These software products are *free*, both in the sense
of monetary cost and in the sense of liberty.

If you're still not convinced, just look up the environmental
destruction caused by Big Tech. Data centers used 485 TWh in 2025, and
it's projected to double by 2030.[^IEA-Energy-and-AI]

Android users should use
[ICSx⁵](https://f-droid.org/packages/at.bitfire.icsdroid)
to sync the calendar. This enables calendar synchronization alongside
any calendar application.

*Due to Apple's extreme hostility toward open standards and software
freedom, there are literally zero open source calendar apps for iPhone
on the App Store.*
